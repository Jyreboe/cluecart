# Three 20-second demo scripts

All visuals must retain the “Demo data” label. Do not imply connected stores or conversion results.

## 1. Find the question (20 seconds)

0–5s: Open Forma from Products. “Your customers ask practical questions.”
5–12s: Select Dimensions & scale; show the three exact excerpts. “Cluecart’s demo groups related questions and keeps the original words in view.”
12–20s: Show the confirmed dimensions beside the editable draft. “Review the evidence. Then make the answer easier to find.”

## 2. Keep the sources (20 seconds)

0–5s: Open Add evidence and choose a fictional CSV. “Bring the customer’s words.”
5–12s: Map the text column and show the redacted preview. “Review the records before adding them. Duplicate text is removed; other products stay out.”
12–20s: Import and view Evidence. “Specific questions. Traceable sources. A clearer place to start.”

## 3. Make a considered change (20 seconds)

0–6s: Edit the Forma clarification. “A recommendation is a draft, not a decision.”
6–12s: Check the facts and approve the demo version. “You check the details. You approve the change.”
12–20s: Export HTML and open Changes. “Export your copy and keep the evidence snapshot. This demo does not publish to Shopify.”
